# Decision

The UnitRender investigation chain is archived as a successful causal-narrowing campaign even though no new UnitRender production optimization has yet been retained.

The formal engineering outcome is that multiple tempting redesigns were eliminated before broader complexity was introduced. The remaining target is now narrow enough to justify a low-blast-radius E5-4 candidate around per-hex derived world-coordinate payload reuse.

This synthesis should be cited when future performance work is tempted to jump directly from subsystem-level cost to structural rewrite.
